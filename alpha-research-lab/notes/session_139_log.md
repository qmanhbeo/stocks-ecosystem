## H509 Design Pass: Latent-State Discovery Without Symbolic Labels (2026-05-10)

## Objective

Define one scientifically disciplined H509 implementation that tests whether the
H507/H508 ontology survives when C23/freshness/cofire/carry/low-C16 symbolic
labels are removed from state construction.

Scope discipline:
- structural interpretability only
- no portfolio optimization
- no threshold sweeps
- no `2026+` usage

## Candidate approaches considered

### 1) Emission-space clustering + transition audit (recommended)
- What it tests:
  - whether unsupervised clusters from observable microstructure emissions
    naturally recover quiet/onset/amplified/carry/C24-like channels.
- Minimal method:
  - build ticker-day emission matrix from observable features only
    (illiquidity, flow pressure, volatility expansion, ceiling proximity,
    participation proxies, short-horizon lag context)
  - fit clustering on dev years only, assign labels to validation/confirmation
  - estimate per-ticker next-day transition matrix, persistence, and state IC.
- Success:
  - low-dimensional cluster set with stable transition topology across phases,
    non-trivial persistence, and IC separation from quiet in most years.
- Failure:
  - unstable cluster identities across phases, no coherent transition ecology,
    or only sparse/high-variance "wins."
- Artifacts:
  - clustered panel parquet, transition matrices by phase/year, state
    prototypes (z-scored centroids), and year-stability IC table.
- Hallucination risk:
  - k-choice artifacts, scale-sensitive clustering, and spurious clusters driven
    by feature magnitude rather than dynamics.

### 2) Gaussian HMM / Markov-switching emissions (high-upside, higher risk)
- What it tests:
  - whether latent states inferred with explicit temporal continuity produce a
    coherent hidden regime machine better than static clustering.
- Success:
  - stable hidden-state occupancy + transition pattern on out-of-sample years
    with reproducible emission signatures.
- Failure:
  - state collapse, label switching instability, or overfit transitions that do
    not transfer to validation/confirmation.
- Artifacts:
  - hidden-state posterior panel, transition matrix, emission means/covariances,
    phase-wise likelihood + IC diagnostics.
- Hallucination risk:
  - EM local minima and overfitting; hidden states can look interpretable while
    being mostly estimation artifacts.

### 3) Manifold-first embedding + regime segmentation (safest for compression)
- What it tests:
  - whether one or two continuous latent coordinates explain most surviving
    structure, with regimes as segmented zones on that manifold.
- Success:
  - compressed manifold with clear regime occupancy and smoother transitions
    than symbolic states.
- Failure:
  - no stable geometry, weak reproducibility, or segmentation instability.
- Artifacts:
  - 2D embedding coordinates, segmented zones, trajectory summaries, phase
    stability diagnostics.
- Hallucination risk:
  - projection distortions; pretty geometry that is not temporally robust.

## Structural claim boundaries

- Structural evidence (allowed): reproducible state occupancy, persistence,
  transitions, overlap/containment with known emissions, and IC separation.
- Causal claims (not proven): mechanisms like "institutional absorption" or
  "queue friction cause alpha" remain hypotheses.
- Deployability claims (out of scope): no direct conclusion on tradability,
  costs, or capacity.
- Portfolio claims (out of scope): no top-k/rebalance optimization interpretation.

## H509 recommendation

Run **Approach 1** first as the concrete H509 implementation.

Why this path:
- closest to current evidence base and available tooling
- lowest infrastructure risk under current constraints
- directly falsifiable against H507/H508 ontology
- naturally produces compression outputs needed for H510/H511 decisions

Minimum viable H509 spec:
- data window: 2015-2025 only
- split: dev train fit, frozen transform on validation/confirmation
- features: observable emissions only; exclude symbolic C23/freshness/cofire/
  carry/low-C16 state flags from clustering inputs
- model sweep: small predeclared `k` set (e.g., 4/5/6) only
- acceptance bar:
  - at least one non-trivial quiet-vs-active separation with stable state
    prototypes across phases
  - coherent transition directionality (active -> carry-like -> quiet tendency)
  - state-level yearly IC not driven by one sparse pocket
- failure bar:
  - topology not stable, or compression does not beat a naive random partition,
    or all active states collapse into one noisy blob.

## H509 Interpretation Audit (2026-05-10, skeptical pass)

## What H509 actually discovered (plain language)

For selected `k=6`, the latent map is best read as:
- a **large quiet background split into multiple low-IC quiet subclusters**
  (`cluster 0/1/5`)
- a **distinct C24 queue-friction channel** with a two-step pattern:
  - `cluster 2`: current-day ceiling impulse state
  - `cluster 3`: lagged ceiling persistence / queue follow-through state
- one **high-IC illiquid low-pressure stress pocket** (`cluster 4`) that is
  strongly associated with high illiquidity plus strongly negative flow-pressure
  signatures (low pressure rank, negative imbalance), not primarily ceiling.

## Key skeptical clarifications

1. **Coarse vs full ontology rediscovery**
- H509 clearly rediscovers coarse quiet-vs-active structure and a convincing
  C24-like channel.
- It does **not** cleanly rediscover onset/amplification/carry as separate
  one-to-one latent states for the broader C23-family manifold.

2. **C24-like evidence quality**
- C24 mapping is the strongest post-hoc symbolic match
  (`jaccard ~0.316-0.364`, symbolic-in-cluster containment up to `~0.966`).
- Transition shape supports a queue sequence (`cluster 2 -> 3` dominant).

3. **Entanglement explanation**
- Onset/amplification/carry overlap is likely partly real (same manifold) and
  partly representation-limited (current feature set and KMeans geometry do not
  separate those submodes cleanly).

4. **Potential artifact risks**
- KMeans with Euclidean geometry appears to split the quiet mass into multiple
  nearby clusters; semantic separation among quiet subclusters is weak.
- Rare ceiling features carry very high standardized loadings and can dominate
  geometry for ceiling-associated clusters.
- Missingness is low overall but uneven by cluster (e.g., higher lag-feature
  missingness in quiet-heavy clusters), so weak quiet subcluster differences
  may partly reflect data-availability structure.
- Important protocol caveat: the `k` choice (`best_k=6`) is based on a summary
  score that uses full-period IC diagnostics, so `k` selection itself is not a
  purely dev-only model-selection step. Treat this as interpretation support,
  not a finalized latent contract.

## Durable interpretation boundary

Durable:
- latent organization is real beyond random partitions
- quiet-vs-active separation is robust
- C24-like queue-friction channel is structurally meaningful
- one high-IC illiquid low-pressure active pocket is repeatedly detected

Not durable yet:
- exact onset/amplification/carry decomposition in unsupervised space
- strong claim that `k=6` is uniquely correct
- causal mechanism claims
- any deployability/portfolio claim
