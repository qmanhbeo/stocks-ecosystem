## Confirmed Fixes

### 1. Feature-engineering divisions now degrade to `NaN` instead of `inf`

Fixed in `alpha-research-lab/src/feature_engineering.py`.

Changes:

- guarded Bollinger `%B` with a zero-safe band-width denominator
- guarded `vol_ratio` with a zero-safe rolling volume denominator
- reused the guarded rolling volume mean for `vol_trend`

Why this mattered:

- flat-price windows can make Bollinger upper and lower bands collapse to the same value
- zero-volume windows are realistic for halted or illiquid VN tickers
- previous behavior could emit `inf` into feature columns and contaminate downstream matrices or model inputs

### 2. Walk-forward year skips now emit diagnostics

Fixed in:

- `alpha-research-lab/walk_forward.py`
- `alpha-research-lab/ensemble_wf.py`

Changes:

- added explicit `print(...)` output before continuing when a year is skipped for insufficient train/test samples

Why this mattered:

- earlier behavior silently dropped years from output tables
- missing years could be mistaken for successful evaluation coverage instead of data scarcity

### 3. RL reset metadata now reports inclusive episode length

Fixed in `RL-trading-agent/vn_stock_rl/env/base.py`.

Change:

- corrected `total_steps` from `end_idx - start_idx` to `end_idx - start_idx + 1`

Why this mattered:

- the env terminates only after `current_step > episode_end_idx`, so the decision range is inclusive
- previous reset metadata under-reported episode length by one step
